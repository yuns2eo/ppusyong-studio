# PPUSYONG — OFFICE LOOK GUIDE

## Purpose

Use this file **only** for scenes in which PPUSYONG is going to work, working in an office, attending a meeting, giving a presentation, taking an office break, or leaving work.

Read `PPUSYONG_Character_Lock.md` first. That universal identity lock always has higher priority. This file adds a removable office look; it does not change PPUSYONG's character, anatomy, fur, or face.

Use the supplied office turn-around reference images as the visual source of truth whenever they are attached.

---

## Office identifiers

Apply the following three recognisable office items.

### Round glasses

- thin black, round wire-frame glasses
- small and centered naturally over the sleepy eyes
- follows the face angle and may be partly hidden in a side or back view

Do not use thick frames, sunglasses, a different frame color, or oversized glasses.

### Employee ID card

- a small white or translucent rounded-rectangle ID holder
- a thin beige lanyard with a small top-center clip
- a tiny PPUSYONG portrait inside the card
- hangs naturally on the upper chest and follows gravity and pose

Tiny lettering does not need to be readable in an AI-generated image. Do not turn the ID into a large blank card or unrelated badge.

### Beige crossbody bag

- a petite beige leather crossbody bag
- a thin beige strap crossing the body naturally
- a small fluffy PPUSYONG keychain attached to the bag
- remains small relative to PPUSYONG's compact body

The bag may be partly hidden or positioned behind the body depending on the pose and view, but its strap and design should remain recognisable when visible.

---

## Office-scene rule

Office identity comes only from these removable accessories and the named office activity. PPUSYONG may sit, walk, commute, drink coffee, use a computer, present, or meet colleagues.

Do not change the core face, head-to-body ratio, fur texture, hair curl, ears, tail, limb length, or body volume to make the character look more professional or human.

---

## When not to use this file

Do not apply this look for home, travel, exercise, sleep, or other non-office scenes unless the scene explicitly calls for PPUSYONG to wear the office items.

For those scenes, use only `PPUSYONG_Character_Lock.md` plus a natural-language scene request.
